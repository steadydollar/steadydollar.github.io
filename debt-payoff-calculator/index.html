How the debt snowball works

Pay the minimum on every debt. Put every extra dollar on the smallest balance. When it's gone, add its payment to the next smallest. Each payoff makes your payment bigger, like a snowball rolling downhill.

Snowball vs. avalanche

The avalanche method pays the highest interest rate first. It always costs the same or less interest. The snowball gives you quick wins, and for a lot of people that's what keeps them going. 3 ways to reach your date faster

  • Try a bigger extra payment. Change the extra amount above from $100 to $150 and watch the date move.
  • Stop new charges. The math only works if the balances stop growing.
  • Use "found" money. Tax refunds and three-paycheck months can knock out a whole debt. See how to use your 3-paycheck months.

FAQ

What if I can only pay the minimums?

Set the extra amount to 0. You'll see how long minimums alone take. Then try small amounts, like $20, to see how much time each one saves.

Does this include my mortgage?

Most people leave the mortgage out and focus on credit cards, personal loans, medical bills and car loans first.
